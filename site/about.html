About us

Moving Africa forward, one mile at a time

For more than twenty years our team has moved cargo across Africa's most demanding roads, from our base in Ndola on the Zambian Copperbelt.

Who we are

Your partner in logistics

We are a team of logistics specialists who have moved diverse cargo across Africa's challenging landscapes for more than two decades. Our experience lets us deliver efficient, tailored transport for vital sectors, including mining, manufacturing, construction and energy. Whether it is raw materials or industrial cargo, we get your goods to their destination safely and on time.

At the core of our approach is the long relationship. Our service does more than move goods. It supports progress across the continent. With us as your logistics partner, you gain a carrier that is invested in your growth.
