Privacy policy

How Patriot Africa collects and uses personal data on this website.

This privacy policy explains how Patriot Africa and its affiliated entities ("we", "us", "our") collect and use personal data on our website and on any mobile application that displays this policy. We care about the privacy of your personal information and we are committed to protecting it. This policy explains how we collect, use, disclose and safeguard your information when you visit https://patriotafrica.com. Please read it carefully. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the site.

1. What information do we collect?

We collect personal information that you give us voluntarily, such as:

  • Name
  • Company name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Details of the cargo and route in a quote request

We collect this information when you fill out a form on the website or contact us directly.

2. How do we use your information?

We use the personal information you give us to communicate with you about the logistics services you ask about. This includes replying to your enquiry, discussing your cargo transport needs and preparing quotes. We may also tell you about updates, offers or changes to our services that may be relevant to your business.

We process your personal data on the following lawful grounds:

  • Consent. When you send a quote request, you consent to us processing your data to reply to it and to provide the services you ask for.
  • Performance of a contract. If you engage our services, we need to process your personal data to perform that contract.

Where applicable, we comply with the UK GDPR and other data protection laws when we process your information.

3. How long do we keep your information?

We keep your personal information for as long as we need it for the purposes in this policy, including providing our services and meeting legal obligations. The period depends on the nature of the information, legal requirements and our business needs. When we no longer need your information, we delete or anonymise it securely. If you want us to delete your information sooner, contact us and we will handle your request in line with applicable law.

4. What are your privacy rights?

You have the right to access, update or delete the personal information we hold about you. You can ask us to restrict or stop processing your data, or object to how we use it. If you consented to marketing messages, you can withdraw that consent at any time. You can also ask for a copy of your personal data or ask us to transfer it to another provider. To use any of these rights, contact us with the details below. We will respond in line with applicable data protection law.

5. How do we keep your information safe?

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorised access, loss, misuse or alteration. These include secure servers, encryption and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute protection, but we review and improve our security practices continuously.

6. International data transfers

We may transfer your personal data to, and process it in, countries outside your country of residence. When we do, we put appropriate safeguards in place to protect it, in line with applicable data protection law.

7. Cookies

This website does not use advertising cookies. If we add analytics, we will update this policy and ask for your consent where the law requires it.
