Load types

Full load or part load

LTL

Part load

Less than truckload. Your consignment shares a truck with other compatible cargo, so you pay only for the space you use.

  • Smaller or regular shipments
  • Lower cost per consignment
  • Palletised, bagged and packaged goods

FTL

Full load

Full truckload. A dedicated truck carries only your cargo, from your gate to the destination, on your schedule.

  • Large, heavy or high value consignments
  • Fewer handling points
  • Direct transit across borders
